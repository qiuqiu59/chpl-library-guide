📖 CHPL (Chapel Hill Public Library) Story

Why Is CHPL (Chapel Hill Public Library) Losing Money For Books & Tech?

CHPL (Chapel Hill Public Library) is an awesome community hub where anyone can read books, borrow laptops, use free computers, and join fun reading programs. But right now, even though CHPL's total budget looks big ($4.98 Million), almost all of it goes to rising staff healthcare & bills, leaving less money for actual books and tech lending. Here is what is happening and how we can help fix it!

What Is Happening To CHPL (Chapel Hill Public Library)?

Based on the official budget file, here are the 3 main reasons CHPL is feeling squeezed.

Problem 1
🏥

Rising Healthcare & Staff Costs at CHPL

Health insurance went up by 6.0% and retirement went up by 0.75%. Because CHPL must pay these bills first for all the librarians, almost no new money is left for new books.

Problem 2
📉

CHPL Special Grants Dropped to $0

In previous years, CHPL received $80,000+ in special grants to buy cool stuff. In the 2025-26 budget, grant money dropped all the way to $0!

Problem 3
✂️

Less Tech, Books & Staff Hours at CHPL

CHPL had to cut its collection gift budget by 18.8%, reduce specialized staff from 4 down to 3.5 positions, and technology lending dropped from 1,925 devices to just 671.

Solution Plan

The 4-Step Plan to Save CHPL

A simple, realistic plan to keep CHPL (Chapel Hill Public Library) awesome for years to come.

Step 1

Get Grants & Fix CHPL Tech Lending

Right Now (Months 1–3)
▼
📖
Apply for CHPL Book & Literacy Grants

Submit applications for state and non-profit grants so CHPL doesn't have $0 in grant money.

💻
Fix CHPL Laptop & Wi-Fi Hotspot Lending

Renew Wi-Fi hotspots and get replacement laptops so kids and families can borrow tech again.

Step 2

Ask Orange County for Fair CHPL Funding

Next (Months 3–6)
▼
🤝
Show County Leaders How Many People Use CHPL

Lots of people living outside the town borders love CHPL. The county should help chip in fairly so town residents don't pay for everything alone.

Step 3

Save Money on E-Books & Rent CHPL Rooms

Months 6–12
▼
📱
Team Up with Other NC Libraries for Cheaper E-Books

Share digital audiobook and e-book licenses with nearby libraries to get discounts for CHPL.

🏢
Rent CHPL Rooms During Closed Hours

Let community groups or businesses rent CHPL meeting rooms in the evenings to make extra revenue.

Step 4

Build a Long-Term CHPL Endowment Fund

Year 2 and Beyond
▼
🌱
Support Friends of CHPL Book Sales & Endowment

Grow a permanent fund that earns interest every single year to buy books for CHPL forever.

Get Involved

How Anyone (Even Kids!) Can Help CHPL

You don't have to be a politician or rich to make CHPL thrive. Here are easy ways to help:

📚

1. Visit & Borrow Lots of Books at CHPL!

When you check out books, audiobooks, and graphic novels at CHPL, official circulation numbers go up, proving to local leaders that CHPL is super important.

🛍️

2. Shop at Friends of CHPL Book Sales

Buy cheap books at the Friends of CHPL used book sales. 100% of that money goes straight back into buying new materials for CHPL!

🌟

3. Join CHPL Summer Reading & Programs

Sign up for CHPL Summer Reading, attend storytimes, coding workshops, and art events. Bringing friends helps keep attendance numbers high!

🙋

4. Volunteer Your Time at CHPL

Volunteers and teens contribute over 3,000+ volunteer hours every year to help shelve books and prep craft kits at CHPL.

🗣️

5. Tell Your Family & Neighbors About CHPL

Remind your parents and friends about all the free resources at CHPL: free Wi-Fi, 3D printers, study rooms, and state park passes!

🎁

6. Donate Used Books to Friends of CHPL

Drop off gently used books and games at the CHPL donation bin for the next big book sale.
